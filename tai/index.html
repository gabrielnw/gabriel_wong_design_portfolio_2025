Tuskegee Airmen Inc.

Overview

A redesign of the Tuskegee Airmen Inc. website so visitors of every age can find programs, donate and sign up, plus social media templates to reach younger audiences.

Year
2023
Length
4 months
Role
Designer, team of 6
Methods
UX/UI design, social media

Read the full process

Question

Tuskegee Airmen Inc. preserves the legacy of African Americans who served in the Army Air Corps in World War II. Its website had a lot of information but no clear structure. Older visitors struggled to navigate it, and younger ones had little reason to engage. How could the site make the important paths, like donating and signing up, obvious?

Process

We ran a competitive analysis, surveyed visitors of different ages, and did card sorting with the board. Board members disagreed on where pages belonged, so we consolidated redundant tabs into four: About, Programs, Press and Get Involved.

We then built a style guide and design system in Figma that kept TAI's red, white and blue, checked for accessibility, and redesigned the densest pages.

Outcome

In timed user testing of the prototype, people navigated 40% faster, and 80% of users agreed with the new main navigation. Signup went from several steps to one click. We also handed over Canva templates and a social media handbook with a content calendar.

Made with Clara Truong, Tyson Cheung, Apoorva Makarla, Caleb Moran and Steve Sheng through Develop for Good.
